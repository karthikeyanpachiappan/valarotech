About us

We help engineering teams deliver with less friction

Valarotech is an IT services company built around one belief: great software should be easy to release, safe to change and simple to run.

Our story

Born from real-world delivery pain

Valarotech was founded by engineers who had spent years on the front line of software delivery: late-night releases, fragile servers, and cloud bills nobody could explain. We saw the same patterns at company after company, and we saw how much changed once delivery was automated and infrastructure was treated as code.

So we built a company to do exactly that for others. Today we partner with startups, growing product companies and enterprise teams to modernise how they build, ship and operate software, with DevOps at the centre of everything we do.

The name reflects that aim: technology that helps teams grow steadily and sustainably.

  1. Start with the painEvery engagement begins by listening to the engineers who live with the problem.
  2. Automate the path to productionBuild, test, scan and deploy without manual hand-offs.
  3. Codify the platformInfrastructure, policy and configuration in version control.
  4. Measure and improveTrack delivery metrics and keep raising the bar.
  5. Hand over with confidenceYour team owns and understands everything we build.
Purpose

Mission & vision

Our mission

To make reliable, secure and automated software delivery accessible to every team, regardless of size, by combining hands-on engineering with practical coaching.

Our vision

A world where releasing software is routine, infrastructure is never a mystery, and engineers spend their time building products instead of fighting fires.

Values

The principles behind our work

These guide how we design systems, how we work with clients, and how we treat each other.

Reliability first

We build systems that behave predictably and fail safely, with rollback always an option.

Partnership

We work alongside your engineers as one team, sharing context, credit and responsibility.

Transparency

Clear estimates, open documentation and honest advice, even when the answer is "you don't need this".

Continuous improvement

We measure, learn and iterate, in our clients' systems and in our own way of working.

Our approach

Practical, incremental and built to last

We avoid big-bang transformations. Instead, we deliver value in small, safe steps: a working pipeline in the first weeks, then steady improvements that compound.

Every change is reviewed, versioned and documented, so your team understands the platform as well as we do.

  • Assess before we automate

    We understand your workflows and constraints before recommending any tools.

  • Everything as code

    Pipelines, infrastructure, policies and dashboards are all version-controlled.

  • Security and cost in every decision

    We weigh risk and spend alongside speed, not after the fact.

  • Teach as we go

    Pairing, workshops and runbooks build lasting capability in your team.
