What we review

Six areas, one clear picture

We focus on the areas that most affect delivery speed, reliability, security and cost.

CI/CD & release process

How code moves from commit to production, where it slows down and where it breaks.

Infrastructure & IaC

How environments are created and changed, and how much is still done by hand.

Containers & Kubernetes

Image hygiene, cluster setup, scaling and deployment strategy.

Security & compliance

Scanning, secrets, access control and the evidence auditors will ask for.

Monitoring & incidents

Alerting, dashboards, runbooks and how quickly issues are found and fixed.

Cloud cost

Where spend goes, what is idle and which savings are low-risk.

Who it is for

A good fit if you are…

  • Startups preparing to scale their platform

  • Teams with slow, manual or risky releases

  • Companies moving to the cloud or to Kubernetes

  • Organisations preparing for a security or compliance review

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no cost and no obligation, and the written plan is yours to keep whether or not you work with us.

Do you need access to our systems?

Not necessarily. Most of the review can happen over a screen-share. If access helps, read-only is enough, and we are happy to sign an NDA first.

How long does it take?

The call takes 30 minutes. You receive the written plan a few business days later.

What happens after the assessment?

Nothing, unless you want it to. If you would like help delivering the plan, we will send an estimate.
